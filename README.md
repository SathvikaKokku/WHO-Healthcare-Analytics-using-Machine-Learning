# WHO-Healthcare-Analytics-using-Machine-Learning
WHO Healthcare Analytics: Leveraging Machine Learning to uncover Global Life Expectancy Factors

Project Focus:

1.Investigate the variations in factors influencing life expectancy across different regions and income groups.
2.Determine the extent of differences in the impact of specific factors on life expectancy among various countries using Regression.
3.Develop targeted interventions for international business and healthcare professionals based on country-specific findings to improve life expectancy outcomes.

Libraries:
Numpy, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib, Scikit-learn, and Statsmodels.
